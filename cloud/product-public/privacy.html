My home

Your home. Your choice.

Keep the personal things personal.

You choose who can help.

An assistant needs your permission before it can update your screen. You choose what it can do and can stop it in My home.

Share a little, not everything.

DisplayOS displays short family updates. It does not need your email password. Only photos you choose to upload can appear here. Ask your assistant to leave private messages and sensitive information off your shared screen.

Your screen forgets old updates.

Family updates have an expiry time. Expired updates disappear even if your screen loses its connection. At night the quiet view shows a clock instead of your family's plans.

Sign in without another password.

Your phone or computer checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. DisplayOS does not receive your fingerprint or face. A safety code can help you return if you lose access. Keep it somewhere private.

You can leave.

In My home, you can download your information, remove a screen, stop an assistant or delete your home and account. Removing a screen clears its information before someone else can add it.

This is a trial.

Do not use an old tablet for sensitive information until its security and reliability have been checked. A commercial service needs a published support contact, retention policy and legal privacy notice before release.
